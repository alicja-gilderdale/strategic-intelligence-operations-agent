# Supported use-case patterns

| Pattern | Example output | Entity type | Source type | Template type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Competitor intelligence | Weekly competitor brief | Competitor | Public web, analyst notes | Intelligence brief |
| Market and customer intelligence | Market signal digest | Segment, customer group | News, reports, CRM notes | Signal digest |
| Regulatory and policy monitoring | Regulatory change brief | Regulator, policy topic | Regulator sites, legal updates | Impact brief |
| Account and stakeholder intelligence | Account briefing | Account, stakeholder | CRM, meetings, public web | Account brief |
| PMO governance drafting | Status report, PID, RAID | Project, programme | Teams notes, documents | Governance artefact |
| Risk and incident briefing | Incident briefing | Incident, risk theme | Logs, service reports, comms | Situation brief |

The same agent supports these by changing registries, templates, sources and allowed tools rather than changing the core orchestration.

# Demo script

1. Open the Control Center and show the empty or configured operational overview.
2. Ask the agent to validate the active workspace configuration.
3. Create a generic intelligence domain and confirm its context profile.
4. Add one entity, one approved source, and one output template.
5. Queue an isolated run.
6. Show the work item moving through the worker workflow.
7. Review the resulting evidence and finding records.
8. Show the synthesized output in review-required state, with the review effort stated up front (must-review, spot-check, auto-accepted).
9. Show the approval email and Teams card.
10. Approve or reject the output and explain the human governance boundary.

Use fictional or approved public examples. Do not demo with customer records or private organizational data.
